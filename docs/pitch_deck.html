01 — Understanding of the Problem Statement

Scaling Content Without Governance Breaks Brand Trust

Growing organizations publish daily across internal teams, external agencies, and generative AI tools—leading to rapid brand drift before anyone notices. Decentralized creation outpaces human review bandwidth, creating three severe failure modes:

Failure Mode 01

Off-Tone Messaging

Decentralized writers default to casual colloquialisms or aggressive hyper-promotional jargon that violates brand persona.

- "Our sick new AI crushes competitors!"
Flagged: Unprofessional tone; contradicts calm, authoritative guideline.
Failure Mode 02

Unsupported Claims

Drafts incorporate unverified superlatives, outdated pricing, or unbacked performance stats creating immediate legal risk.

- "Guaranteed #1 fastest app with 100% uptime."
Flagged: Unsupported superlative; contradicts SLA Fact Sheet v1.4.
Failure Mode 03

Visual/Accessibility Mismatches

Marketing graphics apply off-palette hex colors, unapproved logo clear-space, or illegible text contrast ratios.

- Contrast Ratio 2.9:1 (Fails AA standard)
Flagged: Uses unapproved accent #FF0055 on light background.
What We Solve: Replacing slow, subjective 48–72 hour manual reviews with an automated, pre-publication brand copilot grounded in official company guidelines. Sub-5s Pre-Flight

07 — Key Technical Challenge & Architectural Solution

Solving AI Hallucinations & Multi-Format Precision

The Core Challenge

Preventing Hallucinations & Mixed-Format Fragility

  • • Rule Hallucinations: Off-the-shelf LLMs invent non-existent brand rules or guess tone preferences without authoritative grounding.
  • • Multi-Format Heterogeneity: Brand assets arrive in plain copy, formatted .docx proposals, and high-res image banners.
  • • Mathematical Fragility: Pure language models cannot accurately calculate exact pixel-level WCAG contrast ratios or logo clear-space.
Uncontrolled LLMs cause creator disputes
Our Architectural Solution

Strict Clause-Level RAG + Hybrid Vision Math

  • • Strict Clause-Level RAG: Brand books are chunked with metadata (Document Title, Section, Page Number) in pgvector. Gemini is constrained to cite retrieved chunks verbatim or output "No Rule Found."
  • • Hybrid Deterministic + Multimodal Pipeline: Python (Pillow) computes exact WCAG contrast math and hex clusters, while Gemini Vision evaluates layout context and logo integrity.
  • • Explainable Evidence Linking: Every negative flag surfaces the exact rule quote and suggested rewrite without auto-overriding human authority.
Zero-Hallucination Grounding Guarantee

08 — Responsible Design, Privacy & Human Oversight

Enterprise Confidentiality with Transparent Human Control

Pillar 01

Confidentiality & Data Isolation

  • • Workspace-level Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures unpublished drafts and proprietary brand books remain strictly isolated.
  • • Complete database tenant separation preventing cross-company data leakage.
  • • Zero customer training: Prompts sent to Gemini are governed by enterprise zero-data-retention parameters.
  • • Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
Tenant-Isolated Encryption
Pillar 02

Human-in-the-Loop Guarantee

  • • BrandPulse AI operates strictly as an advisory copilot—never as an autonomous editor or publisher.
  • • AI never auto-edits or auto-publishes content without explicit human confirmation.
  • • Content Creators, Leaders, and Managers retain 100% override and approval authority.
  • • All human overrides and approvals are immutably logged for governance auditing.
Human Agency Safeguard
Pillar 03

Explicit AI Transparency

  • • Every generated audit report prominently displays an unambiguous "Evaluated by Google Gemini AI" badge.
  • • AI suggestions are logged and visually demarcated separately from human peer edits.
  • • Audit trails track model version, timestamp, and guideline revision used during verification.
  • • Full provenance ensures trusted organizational governance.
Auditable AI Provenance
